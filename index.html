Friday · 26 June 2026

Arrival & South Kensington Welcome

Reconnect over pints at your old stomping grounds

Afternoon Travel

Arrival & Check-in

Arrive in London and settle into your accommodation. Take a moment to stretch your legs and soak in the familiar South Kensington streets.

Evening Nostalgia

Nostalgic Pub Night

Head back to your old stomping grounds for a relaxed pub evening dedicated to reconnecting and reminiscing. Twenty-five years of stories to catch up on — best done over a proper pint.

Tip: Start at The Queen's Arms (30 Queen's Gate Mews, SW7 5QL), a classic and unpretentious pub just a stone's throw from campus. Afterwards, take a short walk to see the Imperial College Union bars — FiveSixEight and the Beit Bars — for a true nostalgia trip.
02

Saturday · 27 June 2026

Engineering Marvels & Hyde Park Music

From Millennium Bridge to BST Hyde Park — a day of wonder

Morning Engineering

Engineering Walking Tour

Start the day with a celebration of your shared profession. A walking tour focusing on London's engineering landmarks — the Millennium Bridge, Tower Bridge, and the architectural marvels of the City.

Tip: Guided tours are available from Charnowalks or Buro Happold. Don't miss a group photo at the Institution of Civil Engineers (ICE) at One Great George Street, Westminster.
Lunch Dining

Pub Lunch in the City

After your walk, enjoy a well-deserved lunch at a traditional London pub in the City. Refuel before the afternoon's adventures.

Afternoon Nostalgia

A Return to Imperial

Head back to South Kensington for a walk around the Imperial College campus. See how much has changed and what has stayed the same.

Tip: Pre-book a tour of the Queen's Tower, the iconic 287-foot (87m) landmark from the original Imperial Institute. It offers a fantastic view and a unique alumni experience. Contact reunions@imperial.ac.uk.
Evening Live Event

BST Hyde Park Festival

Your weekend coincides with the opening Saturday of BST Hyde Park, located right on your doorstep. This year features country superstar Garth Brooks, along with Zac Brown Band and Ashley McBryde.

Tip: Tickets should be booked well in advance at bst-hydepark.com.
03

Sunday · 28 June 2026

River Views, Historic Engineering & Celebration

Speedboats, skyline views, and a Sunday roast to remember

Morning Adventure

Thames Speedboat Experience

Experience the thrill of a high-speed RIB boat trip on the Thames. An exhilarating ride combining sightseeing with adrenaline — a unique way to see London's riverside landmarks, including the Thames Barrier.

Alternative: For a more historical engineering focus, consider a visit to the Brunel Museum in Rotherhithe to learn about the Thames Tunnel — the first tunnel under a navigable river anywhere in the world.
Lunch Dining

The Great British Sunday Roast

No weekend in London is complete without a proper Sunday Roast. Book a table for your group at a top-rated pub and enjoy roast beef, Yorkshire pudding, and all the trimmings.

Tip: Near your South Kensington base, The Hereford Arms is excellent. For some of London's best, consider Hawksmoor or The Angel Inn in Highgate.
Afternoon Sightseeing

Panoramic City Views

Head up high for a breathtaking perspective of the city you once called home. Sky Garden offers free 360-degree views (book ahead at groups@skygarden.london), or splash out on The View from The Shard — the highest viewing platform in Western Europe.

Evening Celebration

Group Celebration Dinner

For your final evening together, enjoy a special celebration dinner. Consider a restaurant with a private dining room to make the occasion truly memorable.

Tip: The Other House in South Kensington has an intimate private dining room for 8 (the Mervyn Peake room). For a grander experience, consider Daphne's in South Kensington or one of Gordon Ramsay's restaurants.

Monday · 29 June 2026

Wimbledon & Farewell

Day One of the Championships, then a final brunch together

Morning Live Event

The Wimbledon Experience

Your trip coincides with the first day of the Wimbledon Championships. The famous Wimbledon Queue is an experience in itself — arriving early could secure you a Grounds Pass to wander the outside courts and watch on Henman Hill.

Tip: Alternatively, soak up the atmosphere at a nearby pub showing the matches live.
Lunch Dining

Farewell Brunch

Enjoy a final meal together before heading your separate ways. Raise one last glass to 25 years of friendship and the memories you've made this weekend.

Tip: The Wolseley on Piccadilly is a classic grand café experience. For something more relaxed, try one of the many brunch spots in Covent Garden or Soho.
Afternoon Travel

Departures

Head to the airport or station for your journey home, filled with new memories and re-kindled friendships. Until the next reunion!

Where It All Began

The Queen's Tower &
Your Imperial Legacy

Standing at 287 feet, the Queen's Tower is the last remaining structure of the original Imperial Institute, built in 1893. It has watched over generations of Imperial students — including your class of 2001.

Alumni can arrange a guided tour to climb the 324 steps to the top, offering panoramic views across London. It's the perfect backdrop for a group photo that marks a quarter-century since you walked these grounds as students.

Contact the Alumni office at reunions@imperial.ac.uk to arrange your visit.

A British Tradition

The Sunday Roast

No reunion weekend in London would be complete without gathering around a table for a proper Sunday Roast. Crispy Yorkshire puddings, perfectly roasted beef, golden potatoes, and rich gravy — it's the taste of home.

Book a long table at a top gastropub and let the conversation flow. After 25 years, there's plenty to talk about.
